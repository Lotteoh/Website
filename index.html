Fluency is a skill you use, not a certificate you earn

Rødgrød is a language consultancy for anyone who wants to live fully in Danish or English — professionals navigating meetings and negotiations, partners settling into a new life here, or simply people who want to speak with more ease and confidence. I coach the language you'll actually use: at work, at the school gate, with neighbours, in the conversations that make a place feel like home.

Every session starts from where you are. No fixed syllabus. Just a clear path from your current level to what you want to achieve.

About

Hello, I'm Charlotte

Why Rødgrød? Ask any non-Dane to say "rødgrød med fløde" and watch them try — it's a beloved Danish dessert and, unofficially, a rite of passage for anyone learning the language.

Language is more than grammar and vocabulary. At its core, it's about communication and connection — and the ability to master the language whether you're at the school gates, in a meeting, or simply finding the words to express who you are.

Native Danish, I lived, worked, and raised a family in London for 25 years before relocating back to Denmark. I have spent 18 years teaching language at an international school in Denmark — an experience that gave me a firsthand, unique insight into navigating a new language and culture, both in everyday life and the workplace. That experience became the foundation for Rødgrød.

I teach linguistics. But more than that, I coach confidence. My approach is bespoke, built around your words, your context, and your vision for where language should take you.

Alongside teaching, I also translate — across English and Danish. Recent projects include translating a company website into English and translation work for a museum.

  • Native Danish, fluent in English
  • 25 years living and working in London
  • 18 years teaching in international school
