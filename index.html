I work with people who need the language for real life, not for a textbook: professionals who have to hold a meeting, partners who've moved here for someone else's job and people who want to stop translating everything in their head — at the school gate, with neighbours or wherever it comes up.

About

Hello, I'm Charlotte

Why Rødgrød? Try asking a non-Dane to say "rødgrød med fløde" — it's a Danish dessert, and being able to say it correctly is a rite of passage if you're learning the language.

Language isn't just grammar and vocabulary — it's how you connect with people, whether that's at the school gate, in a meeting, or trying to say what you mean.

I'm Danish, but I lived, worked and raised a family in London for 25 years before moving back to Denmark. I've spent 18 years teaching language at an international school here, and it gave me a real sense of what it's like to navigate a new language and culture — not just in the classroom, but at work and in everyday life. That's where Rødgrød came from.

I teach linguistics, but most of what I'm doing is helping people feel confident using it. Every course is built around you and your language needs.

I do translation too, between English and Danish. Lately that's included translating a company website into English, and some work for a museum.

  • Native Danish, fluent in English
  • 25 years living and working in London
  • 18 years teaching in international school
